# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

PUBG Tracker collects and processes only the information needed to provide its Discord bot features. This may include:

- **Discord server (Guild) IDs and channel IDs:** Used to save each server’s configuration and send requested or scheduled reports to the correct channel.
- **Discord user IDs and usernames:** Used only where needed to respond to commands, apply permissions, or associate a report display with a Discord member.
- **Command inputs and configuration settings:** Such as tracked PUBG player names, reporting channels, selected ranked queue, scheduled report times, clan-report settings, and weekly report preferences.
- **Public PUBG account and game-statistics data:** Such as PUBG player IDs, player names, match activity, lifetime statistics, ranked standings, clan information available through the official PUBG API, and relevant game progression data.
- **Limited report cache data:** The Bot may store the names of tracked players with ranked activity in a selected queue. This helps reduce repeated PUBG API requests and improves report speed.

## 2. How We Use Information

The information is used strictly to:

- Provide PUBG statistics, clan reports, ranked reports, activity reports, leaderboards, and scheduled updates requested by a Discord server.
- Save each server’s Bot settings and tracked-player list.
- Reduce unnecessary PUBG API requests through temporary or persistent report caching.
- Respond to commands, manage permitted configuration actions, diagnose errors, and maintain reliable operation.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot stores server-specific configuration and tracked PUBG player information needed for its features. This can include server IDs, channel IDs, player names, PUBG identifiers and statistics returned by the official PUBG API, report schedules, and cache information.

Data is retained only while it is needed to provide the Bot’s functionality. Server administrators may remove tracked players or change Bot settings using the available commands. If the Bot is removed from a server, its stored server-specific data may be removed during normal maintenance.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

PUBG Tracker uses the Discord API to operate within Discord and the official PUBG API to retrieve public PUBG-related data. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

The Bot does not sell, rent, trade, or transfer personal information to advertisers or other commercial third parties. Information is only transmitted to Discord and PUBG when necessary to operate requested Bot features.

## 5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored configuration data and API credentials from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No system can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to what is required to operate and maintain the Bot.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

PUBG Tracker is intended for use through Discord and PUBG accounts. Users should meet the age and account requirements of Discord, PUBG, and any applicable laws in their region.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot gains features or its data practices change. Updates will be posted with a revised effective date.

## 8. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Bot’s developer or use the community support channels associated with PUBG Tracker.
